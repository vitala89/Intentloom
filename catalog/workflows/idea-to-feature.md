# Idea to feature

Turn an idea into a bounded feature proposal.

## Procedure

1. Inspect relevant project context and use `aif-task-router` to decide whether
   focused discovery is warranted.
2. When material ambiguity exists, use `aif-feature-discovery` to ask one
   high-value question at a time and compare two or three viable approaches.
3. Capture problem, users, constraints, non-goals, selected approach,
   architecture and data boundaries, failure modes, acceptance criteria, tests,
   risks, unknowns, and the decision whether to proceed.
4. Review the brief before planning. Do not implement from unresolved discovery.
5. Stop when the stated output is reviewed or a blocking decision is recorded.
